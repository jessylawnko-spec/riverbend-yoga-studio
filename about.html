Meet Our Instructor

Kylie Jenner

Kylie has been training in yoga for almost ten years and enjoys helping learners feel comfortable while learning. Her classes focus on safe movement, breath control, and establishing a supportive environment.

Accessibility

We want our classes to be welcoming for everyone. Chairs, yoga blocks, and pose modifications are available for students who need extra support during class.

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive about 10 minutes before class.
  • Wear comfortable clothing that allows you to move.
  • Please keep phones silent during class.
  • Respect other students and their personal space.
